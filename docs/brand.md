# Brand

The Xerify mark is a custom composite: a dark upper `V` and a green inverted `V` attached at the same center. The lower component also reads as a reversed check. Together they form a literal `X`, while the upper `V` joins `erify` on its reading line. The primary line is:

> Ask another provider. Get a clear second opinion.

The descriptor is:

> Lightweight cross-provider verification for the shell and MCP.

## Assets

The selected working geometry is **Attached X**. `assets/logos/source/xerify-exploration-grid.svg` preserves Attached X, Verify-led, and Compact X comparisons; `xerify-master.svg` is the deterministic vector source. The wordmark uses custom SVG outlines and has no font dependency.

Required horizontal, icon-only, black, and white SVG variants live under `assets/logos/`. PNG icon exports at 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, and 1024 px are generated by `npm run assets:logo`; 16 and 24 px QA exports and a white-background contact sheet are generated at the same time.

Owner direction selects green for the lower reversed-check component. Light-background assets use ink `#111827` and green `#15803d`; dark-background assets use `#f9fafb` and `#4ade80`. Strict monochrome assets remain black or white.

## Usage

- Keep horizontal clear space at least 50% of mark height and icon clear space at least 25%.
- Use the full wordmark no smaller than 120 px digital width or 35 mm print width.
- Use the normal icon no smaller than 24 px or 10 mm. Use the optically simplified 16 px asset for favicons.
- Do not separate the shared center, turn the lower reversed check into a normal check, substitute a font `X`, stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, gradient, glow, or recolor components outside the approved ink/green pairs.
- Preserve one-color meaning; an accent is optional only after palette approval.
