# Brand

The Xerify mark is a custom composite. Its ink upper `V` begins the `Verify` reading; an asymmetric
emerald mirrored check meets it at the center. Together they form the `X` in `Xerify`. The primary
line is:

> Ask another provider. Get a clear second opinion.

The descriptor is:

> Lightweight cross-provider verification for the shell and MCP.

## Assets

The owner-approved geometry is the **attached-center asymmetric X**. The upper path is a narrow ink
`V`; the lower path has a short left arm and long right arm so it reads as a mirrored check rather
than a second chevron. `assets/logos/source/xerify-master.svg` is the deterministic vector source,
and `xerify-exploration-grid.svg` records the approved construction. The `erify` wordmark uses
custom SVG outlines and has no font dependency.

Horizontal, icon-only, black, and white SVG variants live under `assets/logos/`. PNG icon exports at
16, 24, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, and 1024 px are generated by `npm run assets:logo`. Icon SVGs use a
padded square canvas so the same deterministic exporter produces uncropped square PNGs at every
size. The command also generates a white-background contact sheet for small-size inspection.

Light-background assets use ink `#0a0a0a` and dark emerald `#065f46`. Dark-background assets use
white `#ffffff` and emerald `#10b981`. Strict monochrome assets remain black or white. The mark
contains no blue.

## Usage

- Keep horizontal clear space at least 50% of mark height and icon clear space at least 25%.
- Use the full wordmark no smaller than 120 px digital width or 35 mm print width.
- Use the standard icon no smaller than 24 px or 10 mm. Use the optically adjusted 16 px asset for
  favicons.
- Keep the upper `V` and lower mirrored check attached at their center.
- Do not symmetrize the lower check, substitute a font `X`, stretch, rotate, outline, shadow,
  gradient, glow, or recolor the components outside the approved ink/emerald pairs.
- Do not infer formal proof from the check shape. Xerify provides a bounded cross-provider second
  opinion.

## Distribution

Brand sources, review material, and the portable `design/` workspace are repository assets, not npm
runtime files. The package allowlist and `.npmignore` exclude them from published tarballs.
