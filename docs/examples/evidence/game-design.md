# Bounded evidence: fictional game production plan

Evidence set: `game-design-2026-08-19`

This is a synthetic planning snapshot for a two-person independent studio.

- `E-G1 — fixed capacity`: Two people are available for ten calendar weeks, with a hard cap of 40
  hours per person per week. Total capacity is therefore 800 person-hours.
- `E-G2 — required launch scope`: The launch definition requires a 12-level single-player campaign,
  online two-player co-op, account creation, cloud saves, matchmaking, a level editor, moderated
  user-generated level sharing, and Windows/macOS builds. The owner states that none of these
  launch features may be removed or deferred.
- `E-G3 — bottom-up estimate`: Campaign and core mechanics: 360 hours. Co-op networking and
  matchmaking: 300 hours. Accounts/cloud saves/backend: 160 hours. Level editor and sharing: 180
  hours. QA, platform packaging, and release work: 120 hours. Total before contingency: 1,120
  person-hours.
- `E-G4 — risk reserve`: The studio requires a 20% contingency reserve for unfamiliar networking
  and platform work, taking the estimate to 1,344 person-hours.
- `E-G5 — team experience`: Neither team member has previously shipped networked multiplayer or a
  moderated user-generated-content service.

The schedule cannot gain capacity through overtime, contractors, feature cuts, or a later date.
