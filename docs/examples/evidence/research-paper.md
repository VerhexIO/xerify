# Bounded evidence: fictional tutoring study

Evidence set: `research-paper-2026-08-19`

This is a synthetic study summary created for a Xerify example. It is not a real paper and contains
no participant data.

- `E-R1 — design`: 84 undergraduate volunteers selected either the tutoring assistant or the
  existing study guide. Assignment was not randomized.
- `E-R2 — immediate outcome`: At the end of two weeks, the assistant group answered 78% of quiz
  items correctly and the guide group answered 66% correctly. The relative difference is about
  18.2%; no confidence interval or adjusted analysis was reported.
- `E-R3 — retention outcome`: No outcome was collected after the two-week quiz. In particular, no
  six-month assessment exists in the supplied evidence.
- `E-R4 — attrition`: 21 of 105 enrolled participants did not complete the quiz. Attrition was 8%
  in the assistant group and 31% in the guide group.
- `E-R5 — scope`: The study was conducted in one introductory course at one university.

The verification claim concerns six-month retention, not the observed two-week quiz score.
